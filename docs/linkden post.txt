Excited to share a project I’ve been building: an **AI Automation & Growth Operations Platform** designed to connect sales, marketing, events, CRM, content, follow-up, and human approvals into one structured workflow system.

The core idea is simple:

**Growth → Build → Operate**

Instead of using AI tools separately, the platform focuses on turning them into connected, reliable business workflows.

The current focus is **Growth Automation**, including:

• Lead and prospect management
• AI-powered lead qualification
• Buying-signal and engagement analysis
• Event-driven outreach workflows
• Personalized follow-up generation
• Meeting intelligence and action-item extraction
• AI-assisted content creation
• Human-in-the-loop approvals
• Workflow execution and monitoring
• CRM updates and activity tracking
• Analytics and growth insights

A key principle of the platform is:

**AI generates → Human reviews → Human approves → Workflow continues**

This keeps important external communication controlled while still taking advantage of AI for speed, personalization, and automation.

From a technical perspective, the platform is built around:

**Frontend:** React + Vite
**Backend:** Python + FastAPI
**Database:** PostgreSQL / Supabase
**AI:** Gemini with optional Groq support
**Workflow Engine:** Trigger → AI Processing → Approval → Action → CRM Update
**Architecture:** Multi-tenant SaaS with authentication, auditability, structured AI outputs, and workflow monitoring

One of the most interesting parts of this project has been moving beyond simple AI prompts and focusing instead on **AI as part of a complete operational system**.

For example:

**Event → Prospect → AI Qualification → Personalized Outreach → Human Approval → Follow-up → CRM Update → Analytics**

The goal is not to replace people with AI.

The goal is to remove repetitive work, improve consistency, surface better insights, and allow teams to focus on higher-value decisions.

I’m continuing to develop the platform toward a broader vision covering:

**Growth** — Sales, Marketing, Events, Content
**Build** — Workflow Architecture, AI Tools, Integrations
**Operate** — Monitoring, Optimization, Documentation, Scale

This project has been a great opportunity to work across **AI automation, SaaS architecture, workflow engineering, backend development, frontend UX, databases, authentication, and human-in-the-loop AI systems**.
